# Third-party data and Task materials

The root licence does not relicense third-party data. Each Task's `RELEASE_METADATA.json` and `task_index.jsonl` record its upstream source and include a parsed licence or permission where publicly verifiable. Rebuildable recipes omit upstream data and require users to read and accept the authoritative upstream terms. Ready-to-use Tasks are limited to material selected for redistribution under the audited per-Task terms. Upstream terms remain controlling.
